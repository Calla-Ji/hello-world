# Bio stellaachenbach

Stella Achenbach is a designer dedicated to the principles of decentralized governance and the creation of an omni-accessible Metaverse. With a passion for reinterpreting beauty within the digital realm, Stella consistently places creativity at the forefront of her work. 
As the founder of The ALANA Project, she empowers fellow creators with innovative Web3 tools, fostering a thriving community of digital creatives. In her current role as the Lead Steward of Unlock DAO, Stella is building an efficient decentralized network of contributors, driving forward the vision of a collaborative and inclusive creator economy.
